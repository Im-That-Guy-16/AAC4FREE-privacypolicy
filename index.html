AAC4Free: talk with pictures

A free AAC app for iPad, made for autistic children and anyone who communicates with pictures. No adverts, no subscriptions, no tracking.

Contact and support

If you need help, find a problem or have an idea, please email localsparksonline@gmail.com. Tell us which iPad you have and what you were trying to do, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

Open the grown-up settingsTouch and hold the Settings button (top right) for 2 seconds, then answer the simple sum.
Get a better voiceSettings → Voice, then follow the steps to download Apple's free premium voices.
Help with accidental tapsSettings → Touch and switches.
Use switchesSettings → Switch scanning.
Add another childSettings → Child → Add a child.
Change the word levelSettings → Word level.
Find more symbolsEdit a card → Choose a picture symbol → Online library.
Print boardsSettings → Print boards.
Share progress with a therapistSettings → Word log and progress.
Move to a new iPadSettings → Back up boards and photos.
Keep your child in the appUse iPadOS Guided Access (iPad Settings → Accessibility).

Which iPads?

AAC4Free runs on iPadOS 15 and later, so older and second-hand iPads work too, including the iPad mini 4 and iPad Air 2.

Free, for good

The leading AAC apps cost hundreds of pounds. AAC4Free was made so that every child can have a voice, whatever their family can afford. It will always be free, with no in-app purchases.
