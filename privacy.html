Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

AAC4Free is a free communication app for children and adults who communicate with pictures. It has been designed from the start to collect no personal data at all.

The short version

  • AAC4Free does not collect, store, share or sell any data about you or your child.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app works offline. It only goes online when a grown-up chooses to search the ARASAAC symbol library or change the skin and hair colours of symbols (see below).
  • Everything you create stays on your iPad.

What stays on the iPad

To work, AAC4Free saves some information on the device itself. None of it is ever sent to us or anyone else:

  • Boards, cards, favourites and the schedule you create.
  • Photos you add to cards, taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library.
  • Sounds you record for cards using the microphone.
  • Settings, such as your child's name, the chosen voice and how the app looks.
  • Recent sentences and how often each card is tapped, used to show history and suggest favourites.
  • An optional word log of words and sentences used, with dates and times, which grown-ups can switch off.

You can delete all of this at any time by resetting the boards in Settings, clearing the history and word log, or deleting the app.

Camera, photos and microphone

AAC4Free only asks to use the camera, photo library or microphone when a grown-up chooses to add a photo or record a sound for a card. Photos and recordings are saved only inside the app on your iPad.

Sharing and backups

Some features let a grown-up choose to send information off the iPad: Back up boards and photos, Print boards, Export the word log, Share boards (Open Board Format) and Share a sentence. These only happen when you tap them, and they use Apple's standard share sheet, so you choose exactly where the file or text goes (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or Messages). AAC4Free never sends anything by itself.

The ARASAAC symbol library

About 400 picture symbols are built into the app. A grown-up can also search the full ARASAAC library of over 13,000 symbols, and change the skin and hair colours of the people in symbols. Only then does the app connect to ARASAAC (arasaac.org), run by the Government of Aragón, to fetch the pictures.

The app sends only the words typed into the search box, the numbers of the symbols needed, and the chosen colours. It sends nothing about you or your child, and no identifiers. The connection doesn't use cookies or keep any record on the iPad. Downloaded symbols are saved on the iPad so they work offline afterwards.

More than one child

Several children can use the same iPad. Each child's boards, photos, recordings, settings and word log are kept separately on the iPad. Nothing is shared between devices.

Children's privacy

AAC4Free is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13, or from anyone else.

Voices

Speech uses the voices built into iPadOS and works offline. If a grown-up sets up Apple's Personal Voice, it is managed by iPadOS and used only on this device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Any change would continue to respect the app's promise: free, with no adverts and no tracking.
